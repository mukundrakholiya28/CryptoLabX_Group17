Cryptography is the practice of secure communication in the presence of third parties called adversaries. It involves techniques to encode messages so that only authorized parties can read them. Throughout history, cryptography has been used in military communications, diplomatic relations, and private business matters. Modern cryptography relies on mathematical algorithms and computational complexity to ensure security. The field of cryptography is essential for protecting sensitive information in our increasingly digital world. Future developments will continue to advance our capabilities in secure communication and data protection.
